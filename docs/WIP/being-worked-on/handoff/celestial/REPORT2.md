# Celestial Trial log analysis: HARD level 85 (win, loss), HARD level 95 (win), NORMAL level 85 (win)

## 1. Summary

Four logs are now covered:
- Round 1: HARD L85 win, and NORMAL L85 win. Same party (OrionAF the Sentinel, Oria the tank).
- Log A: HARD L85 loss. Overload fired, Oria died at 71510, OrionAF died at 72570.
- Log B: HARD L95 win. The tank was "Tin can" (Warrior, L95, thorns build). OrionAF was the DPS.

Almost everything is exact at tick level:
- **Timing:** boss 583/583 (L95), 137/137 (log A). Players 875/875, 843/843 (L95) and 126/126 (Oria, log A). The Sentinel is 205/205 once Elusive attack speed is included. Round-1 totals still hold.
- **Damage taken:** L95 638/638. Log A 243/243, including Overload. Round 1: HARD 528, NORMAL 159.
- **Damage dealt:** L95 1816/1816. Log A 265/265 Sentinel hits and 126/126 Oria hits. Round 1: 636/636 and 420/420.
- **HP bookkeeping:** log A 2852/2852. L95 14539/14540 (the one miss is the Earthwarden cast row at 66790). Round 1: HARD 3157/3157, NORMAL 1123/1123.
- **Boss HP bookkeeping:** L95 7270/7270, log A 1426/1426 (a separate boss-HP replay got 1118/1118 timestamps), round 1 HARD 3157/3157.
- **Block Fury:** L95 7240/7240, log A 1419/1419, round-1 HARD 3143/3143.

What changed since round 1:
1. **Overload is solved for HARD.** It fires on the spawn tick that brings living adds to 7. It multiplies only the boss's auto-attack, by 6. Add swings are not multiplied.
2. **Tank death and loss are solved.** The killing swing is logged as `player_death`. All enemies retarget the survivor at once and keep their timers. The fight ends when the second player dies.
3. **Bulwark of Thorns is not inert.** Round 1 was wrong. It is fully active for the one "stack player" of a fight. In rounds 1 and A the stack player was OrionAF, who runs Elusive Supremacy. In log B it was Tin can.
4. **Elusive Supremacy works in Celestial.** It adds +3% attack and +3% attack speed per stack.
5. **Scaling by level is solved.** Boss HP and attack come from the party's highest level. Dust ranges use the same level.
6. **Judgment Strike is very likely unblockable.** 0 of 16 were blocked across the 4 logs. It burns no Bulwark and makes no thorns.
7. **Max-HP clamp rules are corrected.** `burden_update` clamps on every change, including add deaths. The bonus-HP clamp (Bulwark and Earthwarden) has its own timing rule.

Still open: Overload on Judgment, Overload end, NORMAL Overload, and the real identity of the "stack player". Profiles for the Sentinel and Tin can are inferred from the logs, not read from files.

Rules that hold across all logs:
1. **Burden is the core mechanic.** n is the number of living adds. It scales max HP, regen, healing, damage taken, DR, block and attack. The HARD and NORMAL configs differ.
2. **Enemies hit the tank.** If the tank dies, they hit the survivor.
3. **Tank damage formula.** `round(A*m*(1-D/100)*(1+dmgTakenPerAdd*n)*ovl)`, with one rounding at the end. D is capped at 75 first, then drained.
4. **The fight clock starts at 500 ms and runs on 10 ms ticks.** The swing interval is `round(1000/AS)`, recomputed every tick.
5. **Players follow the tactics payload.** With focus HOLD and holdCount H, they hit the oldest living add while alive > H, else the boss. Spells and DoTs always hit the boss.
6. **HARD Block Fury.** It is a float. +1 per tank block, cap 60, minus 0.015 per tick. Attack gets `1+0.12*f`.

## 5. What a Celestial port needs beyond the existing PVE engine

**New mechanics**
- A party loop with 2 players, each with their own swing, regen and spell timers, and a death state (HP 0 stays 0; no regen, attacks, spells).
- An enemy list of boss plus adds, with spawn timers, add HP, add swings (random first-swing phase, then 1000 ms), and retargeting when the tank dies.
- The Burden system: n-scaled max HP, regen, healing, damage taken, DR, block and attack. HARD (hpPerAdd 0.04, regen 0.05, heal 0.2, damage taken 0.1, no DR/block drain) and NORMAL (0.1, 0.07, 0.25, 0.2, DR 0.13, block 0.13, attack 0.25).
- Boss and add scaling by `highestLevel` (HARD bands 41..101), including attack and HP formulas and the dust ranges that use the same level.
- HARD Block Fury: a float party stack, +1 per tank block only, 10 ms repeated decay, 60 cap.
- Judgment Strike: a 30 s timer that freezes during paralysis. It is unblockable and takes Bulwark DR.
- **Celestial Overload:** trigger when living adds >= `addsThreshold` (7 HARD, 5 NORMAL); the boss auto-attack is multiplied by `1+attackBuffMultiplier` (6 on HARD). End rule unknown.
- Stack sigils (Bulwark, Elusive, Sunder, Siegebreaker) for exactly one "stack player", with tick-start snapshots for add hits and combined thorns events.
- Earthwarden / Chrono Flux with the /3^fires gain scaling and the 0.7 hit-taken gain.
- The 500 ms start offset and the 10 ms tick.
- The boss spell resist (45 on HARD).
- An add-focus tactic (`focus`, `holdCount`, `bossFocusesTank`, `addsTarget`) for player targeting.
- A result object: dust, glyph drops, key use, and the loss shape.
- A time limit (360 s HARD, 240 s NORMAL).

**Engine changes**
- Regen pulses must use the sheet max HP, not the buffed `maxHp`.
- Lifesteal and the Iron Bastion cast heal must take the healing-reduction multiplier. The cast heal must not be floored when reduced.
- The Iron Bastion cap and the bonus-HP cap must use the Burden-reduced base.
- `burden_update` must clamp to the Burden base on every change (rise and fall), ignoring Iron Bastion, Bulwark and Earthwarden HP.
- The bonus-HP clamp must run only when an enemy swing resolves against the tank, with tick-start stacks. Earthwarden's end must not clamp.
- Cap-then-drain order for DR and block.
- Double hit must not proc on adds.
- Add immunity to Blind and Paralyze (strongly suggested).
- Spell damage must use the rounded `curAtk` and the `*0.55` / max(1, …) form.
- Earthwarden attack must use floor, not round.
- Elusive Supremacy must apply attack and attack-speed per stack and recompute the swing interval every tick.
- Judgment Strike must not burn Bulwark or reflect thorns.
- A single final rounding on tank damage.
- Bulwark of Thorns is NOT inert: grant stacks and thorns to the stack player only, and ignore other players' Bulwark.

**Targeting**
- Enemies target the tank, and retarget the survivor when the tank dies. This needs a tank index.
- Players follow `tactics`: HOLD with `holdCount` H means oldest living add while alive > H, else the boss. Spells and DoTs always hit the boss.
- `add_death.playerIndex` records the last hitter.

**Import**
- The fight loadout must come from `sigilState.equippedSpecies` plus the active mount's glyphs (`activeMountId`), not from the profile presets. The current import picks the wrong sigils, glyphs and attack for OrionAF (2098598 and 1279806 instead of the fight's loadout).
- The importer needs the Sentinel's and Tin can's current profiles (Section 4, items 1 and 2) before their stat sets can replace the inferred ones.

**Validation**
- Use all four logs as golden tests. Checks are tick-exact: HP reconstruction, swing grids, the Block Fury trace, boss HP bookkeeping, thorns, Sunder stacks and the Chrono Flux bar.
- Add log A as the golden test for Overload, tank death, retargeting and the loss result.
- Add log B as the golden test for Bulwark, Earthwarden, Sunder Mark, level-95 scaling and add kill credit.
