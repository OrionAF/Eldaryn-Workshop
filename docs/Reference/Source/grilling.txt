Regarding Sigils.
I will explain how Sigils are obtained, and the whole process around them.
Sigils are forged at the "Sigil Altar".  When you forge a sigil, you earn 1 XP towards the Sigil altar.  All forged sigils earn the same amount of XP towards the Sigil Altar level.  At Sigil Altar level 30, you can evolve the Sigil Altar from Tier 1, to Tier 2.  However, when you evolve your altar, all of your obtained Sigils are destroyed.  But, all Sigils forged on this higher tier Sigil Altar will have boosted base stats.  And once the T2 Sigil Altar has reached level 30 again, it can be evolved to Tier 3.  Which will again destroy all obtained Sigils, but all newly forged Sigils have their base stats boosted even higher.  All sigils can be levelled up to level 30.  When you forge enough duplicate sigils, you can level them up.  All rarities of sigils require the same amount of duplicate sigils to level up.  For example, to level up from level 1 > 2, you need 2 duplicates.  To level up from level 6 > 7, you need 7 duplicates.  It seems like the amount of duplicate sigils needed to level up is the same as the next level.  All sigils within the same rarity groups scale their base stats the same.  For example, all uncommon sigils level 6 on Sigil Altar Tier 2, have Attack of 1,917 and Health of 13,104.  It's currently not publicly known the scaling of the base stats at each Sigil Altar tier and sigil rarity.  It's also not known the scaling of the secondary stats provided by some sigils, or the active effects.  Legendary sigils can not be obtained until Sigil Altar tier 2.  A quick reminder, that the classes have different sigils.  Sentinel has different sigils from Warrior.
